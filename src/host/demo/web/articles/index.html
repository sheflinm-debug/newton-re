The lighthouse is lit again

By Ann Ng, 12 November 2026

After forty years in the dark, the lighthouse on the north point was lit again on Thursday night, its new lamp turning for the first time since 1986.

“It was the first thing I saw from the boat,” said a fisherman, who had waited on the pier with his family, his neighbours and half the town.

What was done

  • The lamp was replaced with a modern one, using a tenth of the power.
  • The stairs, all 142 of them, were repaired by volunteers.

The work cost £120,000, raised by the town over three years. Read how the money was raised.
